The Lady of the Lake is optional: the host chooses whether to play with her.

Who starts with her

The Lady first belongs to the player just before the one who proposes first in round 2, i.e. the player who would propose last in that round.

Using the Lady

After every [[Quest]] except the first, once its result is settled, the player holding the Lady must send her to a player who has never held her. That player shows the holder their team: Good (blue) or Evil (red). Nobody else sees the answer.

The Lady then belongs to the player who was checked. Everyone always knows who holds her and whom she was sent to.

  • She is not used after the Quest that ends the game.
  • A player shows their true team, whatever their role (except Gareth, below): [[Lancelot]] shows Good and [[Mordred]] shows Evil.
  • [[Pellinore]] and [[The Questing Beast]] show Neutral.
  • [[Gareth]] always shows Good, even when Evil.
