• A Reversal card always counts as a Success. It also flips exactly one of the other non-reversal cards: Fail into Success and vice-versa. It always prefers to flip a card that will change the mission outcome. If the mission outcome cannot be changed, then choose arbitrary. If two Reversal cards are played, they cancel out and no effect occurs.
  • For example, on a mission that requires 1 Fail card to fail, if two Fail cards and a Reversal card are played the mission still fails. However, if the same mission required 2 Fail cards to fail, this mission would now succeed as the Reversal changes one of the Fail cards into a Success card.
