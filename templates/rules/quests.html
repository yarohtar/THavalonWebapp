Cards

  • Everyone may play [[Success]] or [[Fail]], Good players included.
  • [[Lancelot]] and [[Maelegant]] may also play [[Reversal]]. [[Agravaine]] must play Fail.
  • [[The Questing Beast]] must play [[Questing Beast Was Here]]. [[Pellinore]]'s cards depend on their sanity.

The first side to 3 Quests wins. If Good gets there, the [[Assassination]] follows.
