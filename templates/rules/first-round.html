The first round has two proposals and a single vote:

  • Once everyone has seen their role, the app names two players to propose, plus a third who will start the [[Proposals]] in round 2.
  • Both proposers choose a team: 2 players in 5–7 player games, 3 players in 8–10 player games.
  • Everyone votes once. Approve picks the first proposer's team and Reject picks the second proposer's team.
  • If the vote is tied, the team goes whose proposer is farther (in proposal order) from the round-2 starter.
  • No meaningful discussion is allowed until every [[Quest card]] for this Quest has been played.
