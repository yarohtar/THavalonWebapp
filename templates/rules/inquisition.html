In games with at least 3 Red players, it is possible for the mission leader to obtain an inquisition ability under certain conditions. Whenever an inquisition ability has been obtained, the mission leader must use it.

Weak Inquisition

Weak inquisition can only be obtained in games with 3 or 4 red players.

  • In games with 3 red players, weak inquisition is obtained the first time the first proposal of the current round is accepted;
  • In games with 4 red players, weak inquisition is obtained the first time one of the first two proposals of the current round are accepted and Strong Inquistion wasn't granted.

When granted, the mission leader chooses a quest card to reveal, but not remove.

Strong Inquisition

Strong inquisition can only be obtained in games with 4 red players. Strong inquisition is obtained the first time the first mission proposal of the current round is accepted. When granted, the mission leader chooses to either:

  • choose a quest card to remove, but not reveal OR
  • choose a quest card to reveal, but not remove.
