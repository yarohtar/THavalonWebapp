When an Inquisition is granted, each team member picks a [[Quest card]] and places it face down. Before the cards are shuffled, the Leader must use the Inquisition, and may choose their own card. A revealed card still counts.

PlayersGranted when
5–6Never.
7–8Weak: the first time the 1st proposal of a round is approved.
9–10Strong: the first time the 1st proposal of a round is approved.
Weak: the first time the 2nd proposal is approved, or the 1st proposal once Strong has been used.

Each kind is granted at most once per game, and never in the [[First Round]].

Weak Inquisition

The Leader chooses one card to reveal.

Strong Inquisition

The Leader chooses one of these:

  • reveal one card, or
  • remove one card without revealing it.

[[Gawain's Inquisition]] is a separate ability and does not count towards these limits.
