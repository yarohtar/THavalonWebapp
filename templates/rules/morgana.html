Ability: Sorceress

On any proposal of Morgana's that isn't the [[Hammer]], Morgana may declare as Morgana instead of proposing. Morgana then chooses the next proposer and reverses the proposal order for the rest of the game.

  • Using the ability counts as one of the round's proposals.
  • Anyone who has already proposed this round, Morgana included, is skipped. Nobody proposes twice in one round.
