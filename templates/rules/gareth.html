Gareth can only be in 9-player games (unless the host changes this), where the host may add them like any other role. Gareth plays on top of the Evil players the host chooses: the usual 9-player game is 3 Evil, Gareth and 5 Good.

Gareth's team

When roles are dealt, a random Good or Evil player is chosen as Gareth's Knight (never a Neutral role). Gareth is on the Knight's team. Then a random player of the other team is chosen as Gareth's Rival.

  • Gareth is told who their Knight and their Rival are, but not their roles, and not Gareth's own team.
  • Gareth wins or loses with their team, like any other player.
  • Gareth may play [[Success]] or [[Fail]], like everyone else.

Who sees Gareth

When Gareth is Evil:

  • The other Evil players see Gareth's player among the Evil players, without being told which of them is Gareth. [[Colgrevance]] sees Gareth's role as usual.
  • Gareth doesn't see the other Evil players.
  • [[Merlin]] sees Gareth as Evil, without being told which player is Gareth.

When Gareth is Good, Gareth is not told who [[Nilrem]] is, since that would give their team away.

The [[Lady of the Lake]] always shows Gareth as Good, even when Gareth is Evil.

Other roles that learn about a team, such as [[Galahad]], [[Arthur]], [[Uther]] and [[Ygraine]], treat Gareth as a member of their real team.

Assassination

When Evil reveal themselves for the [[Assassination]], Gareth is told their team. An Evil Gareth may then reveal themselves and join the discussion. A Good Gareth should stay hidden.

Proposals

For the number of [[Proposals]] and the [[Inquisition]], Gareth counts as an Evil player whichever team they are on. With 3 Evil and Gareth, each round has 5 proposals, with Strong and Weak Inquisition.
