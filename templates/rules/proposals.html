From round 2, players take turns as Leader. The Leader proposes a team and everyone votes. A majority of Approve votes sends the team. If the vote is tied or the team is rejected, the next player proposes.

Each round has one more proposal than there are Evil players:

The last proposal of a round is not voted on. The Leader chooses any team and it goes on the Quest.
