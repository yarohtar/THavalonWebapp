Always appears together with [[Pellinore]], and knows who Pellinore is.

Questing Beast Was Here

The Beast must play this card on every Quest. It counts as a [[Fail]], unless two Quests have already failed or two of these cards have already been played. In that case it counts as a [[Success]].

Points

The Beast wins if it has been on at least one Quest and scores at least 5 points:

  • +1 for each Quest it goes on.
  • +1 for never being on a Quest with Pellinore.
  • +2 if 3 Quests succeed.
  • +3 if Pellinore fails to name it at the end of the game.
