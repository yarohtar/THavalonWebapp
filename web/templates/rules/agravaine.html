Ability: Betrayal

If a Quest with Agravaine on it succeeds anyway, Agravaine may formally declare as Agravaine and make it fail. This can happen when:

  • the Quest needs 2 Fails,
  • a [[Reversal]] was played, or
  • Agravaine's card was removed by a [[Strong Inquisition]].
