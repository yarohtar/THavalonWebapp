If 3 Quests succeed, Evil may discuss, then makes exactly one choice:

  • name one player as [[Merlin]],
  • name one player as [[Nilrem]],
  • name two players as [[the Lovers]], or
  • name no one.

Only players who aren't Evil can be named.

Evil wins if the choice is correct. The role must match: naming Nilrem "as Merlin" does not count. Naming no one is correct only if the game has no Merlin, no Nilrem and no complete pair of Lovers.

The final decision belongs to the highest-ranked Evil player: [[Mordred]], [[Morgana]], [[Maelegant]], [[Agravaine]], [[Colgrevance]], [[Minion of Mordred]], [[Oberon]].

[[Pellinore]] guesses the [[Questing Beast]] before the Assassination.
