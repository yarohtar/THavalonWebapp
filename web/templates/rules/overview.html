THavalon extends Avalon so that every player has a role.

Teams

  • Good wins if 3 [[Quests]] succeed, unless Evil then wins the [[Assassination]].
  • Evil wins if 3 Quests fail, or with a correct Assassination.
  • [[Gareth]] (9 players only) is on a random team and doesn't learn which until the Assassination.
  • [[Balin the Savage|Balin]] (9 players only, instead of Gareth when both could be dealt) is Good, but wins only after playing at least one [[Fail]].
  • Neutral roles ([[Pellinore]] and [[The Questing Beast]], 9 players only) have their own goals.
  • Evil players see each other, except [[Oberon]]. They are told if Oberon is in the game. An Evil [[Gareth]] is seen by the other Evil players but doesn't see them. [[Balin the Savage|Balin]] is seen by the Evil players as one of them.
  • Good players are told who [[Nilrem]] is, if Nilrem is in the game.

Rules the host can change

These pages describe the normal rules. The host can change some of them for a game: which roles can appear at each player count, when lonely roles change, who sees whom (for example whether [[Merlin]] sees [[Mordred]]), [[Gareth]]'s rules, a few card and ability rules, and the [[Assassination]]. If you're in a game whose host changed something, each rules page shows the changes at the top, and the game's summary lists them all.

How a game goes

  1. [[First Round]]: two proposals at once and a single vote.
  2. Later rounds: players take turns making [[Proposals]]. The last one is the [[Hammer]]. Approving an early proposal can grant an [[Inquisition]].
  3. The team plays [[Quest cards]]: [[Success]], [[Fail]] or [[Reversal]].
  4. Optionally, after each Quest from the 2nd, the [[Lady of the Lake]] lets one player learn another's team.
  5. If Good wins 3 Quests, Evil gets the [[Assassination]].

Defaults by player count

The host can change the number of Evil players and the team sizes. Proposals per round and Inquisition follow the number of Evil players.

PlayersEvilProposalsInquisition
5–623none
7–834Weak
93 + [[Gareth]] or [[Balin the Savage|Balin]] (4 without)5Strong + Weak
1045Strong + Weak

Gareth and Balin count as Evil for proposals and Inquisition (Gareth whichever team they are on). Default team sizes for 9 players are 3 · 4 · 4 · 5* · 5, where the fourth Quest needs 2 Fails.

For example, a 9-player game can also be played as 2 Evil + Gareth v 6 others (4 proposals, Weak Inquisition only).
