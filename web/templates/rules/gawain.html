Ability: Inquisition

After a team without Gawain has been chosen and its members have picked their cards, but before the cards are shuffled, Gawain may formally declare as Gawain. Gawain then picks a player on the team, who must reveal their card.

  • Gawain may use this ability on any number of Quests.
  • It happens after any normal [[Inquisition]] and does not count towards their limits.
  • If it reveals a [[Success]], Gawain is Exiled: no more Quests and no more uses of this ability. An Exiled Gawain can still propose and vote.
