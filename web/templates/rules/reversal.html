• A Reversal counts as a [[Success]] and flips one other card: [[Fail]] becomes Success and Success becomes Fail.
  • It always flips a card that changes the result, if one exists.
  • Two Reversals cancel each other out.

Only [[Lancelot]] and [[Maelegant]] can play it.
