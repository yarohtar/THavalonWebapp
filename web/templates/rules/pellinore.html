Always appears together with [[The Questing Beast]]. Pellinore's goal is to hunt the Beast down before it drives them insane.

  • Plays only [[Success]] cards until two [[Questing Beast Was Here]] cards have been played. After that Pellinore is enraged and plays only [[Fail]] cards.

Sanity

Pellinore starts on 0 sanity and wins if no Questing Beast Was Here cards are played, or if they end the game with at least 2 sanity.

  • −1 for each Questing Beast Was Here card Pellinore sees.
  • +1 the first time Pellinore is on a Quest with the Beast.
  • +2 if 3 Quests fail.
  • +3 for correctly naming the Beast at the end of the game. Pellinore guesses before the [[Assassination]] and finds out after it.
