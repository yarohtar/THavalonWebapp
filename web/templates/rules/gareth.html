Gareth's team

{% if not ruleset['Gareth.arthur_knight'] %}

When roles are dealt, a random Good or Evil player is chosen as Gareth's Knight (never [[Arthur]] or a Neutral role). Gareth is on the Knight's team. Then a random player of the other team is chosen as Gareth's Rival.

{% else %} {% call p(True) %}When roles are dealt, a random Good or Evil player is chosen as Gareth's Knight ([[Arthur]] included, never a Neutral role). Gareth is on the Knight's team. Then a random player of the other team is chosen as Gareth's Rival.{% endcall %} {% endif %}
    {% if knows == 'both' %}
  • Gareth is told who their Knight and their Rival are, but not their roles, and not Gareth's own team.
    • {% else %} {% call li(True) %}Gareth is told only who their {{ 'Knight' if knows == 'knight' else 'Rival' }} is (not their role), not their {{ 'Rival' if knows == 'knight' else 'Knight' }}, and not Gareth's own team.{% endcall %} {% endif %}
  • Gareth wins or loses with their team, like any other player.
  • Gareth may play [[Success]] or [[Fail]], like everyone else.

Who sees Gareth

{% if not rules_changed(ruleset, *sight_keys) %}

When Gareth is Evil:

  • The other Evil players see Gareth's player among the Evil players, without being told which of them is Gareth. [[Colgrevance]] sees Gareth's role as usual.
  • Gareth doesn't see the other Evil players.
  • [[Merlin]] sees Gareth as Evil, without being told which player is Gareth.
{% else %}
    {% call li(rules_changed(ruleset, *(sight_keys[2:4] + sight_keys[6:]))) %}{{ gareth_sight(ruleset, 'evil') }} [[Colgrevance]] sees Gareth's role as usual when Gareth is Evil.{% endcall %}

{% endif %}

When Gareth is Good, Gareth is not told who [[Nilrem]] is, since that would give their team away.

{% if ruleset['Gareth.lady'] == 'good' %}

{% else %} {% call p(True) %}The [[Lady of the Lake]] shows Gareth's real team.{% endcall %} {% endif %}

[[Uther]] and [[Ygraine]] treat Gareth as a member of their real team.

Assassination

{% if ruleset.assassination_on %}

When Evil reveal themselves for the [[Assassination]], Gareth is told their team. An Evil Gareth may then reveal themselves and join the discussion. A Good Gareth should stay hidden.

{% else %} {% call p(True) %}There is no [[Assassination]]: Gareth learns their team when the game ends.{% endcall %} {% endif %}

Proposals

For the number of [[Proposals]] and the [[Inquisition]], Gareth counts as an Evil player whichever team they are on. With 3 Evil and Gareth, each round has 5 proposals, with Strong and Weak Inquisition.
